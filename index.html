The method

Five laws every screen obeys.

Most apps treat you like a database to be filled. Parce does the opposite — it teaches the way a great human teacher would, and takes the blame when something doesn't stick.

01

No memorization

Nothing is a list to drill. Everything is built. Discover a pattern, own a hundred words.

02

Audio-first

Eyes free, mouth working. Learn while you walk, drive, or wash the dishes.

03

No failure state

Never "wrong." A missed phrase gets gently re-taught. The responsibility is the teacher's, not yours.

04

Think, then speak

Every prompt is a small puzzle. You pause, assemble it, say it aloud — then hear the model.

05

Long sentences early

Confidence comes from capability. Big sentences are just small known pieces clicked together.

The 90-day journey

Beginner to real conversation — as a trip across Colombia.

~20 minutes a day. Six stages, each one a capability you walk away with — not a topic you sat through. From the Caribbean coast up into the Andes.

1

Arranque · Ignition

Build sentences instantly with cognates + engine verbs (want / can / have to / going to + any verb). Speak in session one.

Days 1–12A0→A1
2

El Presente

Talk about now — regular & key irregular verbs, asking questions, saying no, "doing it right now."

Days 13–30A1
3

Conexiones

The glue of real speech: because, but, so, when, if — plus pronouns (it, to him, for us).

Days 31–48A1→A2
4

El Pasado

Tell what happened. Past tenses learned through contrast, never conjugation tables.

Days 49–66A2
5

El Futuro y los Deseos

Future, "would," and the subjunctive introduced gently as the mood of doubt and wishing.

Days 67–82A2→B1
6

Conversación Real

Idioms, Colombian expressions, speed, register, and unscripted speaking with a real parce.

Days 83–90B1

Milestones

30, 60, 90.

Honest targets at 20 minutes a day. Under-promise, over-deliver.

30
CEFR A1 · ~300 words
  • Café & taxi, handled
  • Ask and answer questions
  • Want / can / have-to / will, any verb
  • Arrive in Medellín 🏅
60
CEFR A2 · ~700 words
  • Tell stories in the past
  • Link ideas: because / but / when / if
  • Market, doctor, plans with friends
  • Arrive in Bogotá 🏅
90
CEFR A2+/B1 · ~1,200 words
  • Hold a ~10-min real conversation
  • Future, "would," wishes
  • Switch formal ↔ parce-casual
  • Parcero de Verdad 🏆

on self_rate(item, rating):
  q = {lost:1, close:3, got_it:5}[rating]
  if q < 3:
    interval = 1
    ease = max(1.3, ease - 0.2)
    flag_for_reteach(item)   // re-explained
  else:
    interval = reps==0 ? 1
             : reps==1 ? 3
             : round(interval * ease)
    ease = clamp(ease + 0.1, 1.3, 2.8)
  reps = (q < 3) ? 0 : reps + 1
  due_date = today + interval
  if reps >= 4 and interval > 35:
    graduate(item)  // kept alive by use
